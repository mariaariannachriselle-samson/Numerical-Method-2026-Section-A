1. SCOPE AND ANALYSIS LIMITATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Rev. 3 defines, applies, validates, combines and visualizes loads.
It does NOT perform stiffness analysis, support reactions, member forces,
displacements, drift, or diaphragm constraint elimination.
The load viewer is a verification layer, not proof of solved structural behavior.
